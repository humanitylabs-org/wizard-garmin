Open Health

Your Body Battery, sleep, HRV, heart rate and activity show in the Health app. This app only keeps your Garmin key and the synced copy.

Connect your Garmin in four short steps. You make a personal key on Garmin Connect and paste it here.

  1. You need the free Garmin Connect account your Garmin watch syncs to. There is no fee: Garmin's Personal API is free for your own data.

    Body Battery, sleep score and HRV status come from Garmin watches that measure them. No account yet? Open Garmin Connect.

    1. Open the page below on Garmin Connect (the website, not the phone app).
    2. Sign in with your usual Garmin email and password.
    Open Garmin Connect
    1. Tap Create Key.
    2. Read and accept Garmin's terms. The key is for your own data on your own box.
    3. Pick how long it lasts. One year means fewer renewals; Garmin emails you a week before it runs out.
    4. Tap Create. Garmin shows the key only once: copy it now, then tap Done.

  4. Key saved. (hidden)

    The app trades the key for a short-lived Garmin sign-in each day. The key stays on this box and is never shown again.

Connection details

Replace Garmin key

Create a new key on your Garmin Connect Personal API Key page and paste it here. The synced history stays.

Disconnect Garmin?

The app deletes its synced copy and stops syncing. Health stops showing Garmin data. Your data on Garmin Connect is not touched. To end the key itself, delete it on your Garmin Connect Personal API Key page.
